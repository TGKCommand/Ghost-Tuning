Policies

Please read these carefully. You confirm them with your signature.

Ghost Tuning By TGK is intended for those owners that want a safe, reliable ECU calibration that is specifically suited to match their vehicle's hardware. This approach to tuning is not intended, nor appropriate, for all owners. This is true custom tuning. Not all owners will be accepted for the opportunity to work with Ghost Tuning. If you want to be considered, please provide the requested information in this application form.

What We Aim To Achieve

With the release of DS1 for the Audi 4.0T Platform, it allows essentially anybody with a computer to custom tune your car. Over the years there has been tuners in and out of the community. This causes alot of harm to the platform as a whole. It stops ingenuity and eventually leads to alot of false information being fed to community. Issues that were never issues in the first place being created under false pretenses eventually leads the community to think its a real issue.

We have had countless customers and supporters ask us for tuning services. We want to bring HEALTHY, custom tuning to the market. We WILL NOT be pushing your motor further than the safety parameters of your given aftermarket hardware, for the sake of trying to get the "Fastest Time", our tuning is catered to the fun daily driveability of the car.

Why Application Process?

We want to ensure brand quality goes across all of our products, including tunes. Limiting to the amount of cars we tune / want to tune allows us to cater TIME and RESOURCES, to the customers that have our products, including tunes. We want to ensure that everybody with our tunes are the ones with the most time invested. Taking on too many cars at once will overwhelm the workload, especially trying to cater to people that want things done at the exact given moment.

We never intended to start custom tuning, nor do we NEED to. Our main focus and what has been fun for US, has been Parts development, and thats what our main company focus is catered around.

We worked extremely hard to build the brand image and name for ourselves. We want to ensure our tuning customers are going to keep the upmost respect and quality of our name.
